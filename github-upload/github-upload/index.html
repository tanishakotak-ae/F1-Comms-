Campaign narrative. Based on the presenter's supplied brief: the campaign supported preparation ahead of race day; Operations supplied updated lists; segments were refreshed daily based on ticket download and wallet behaviour; ready customers could receive transfer guidance; email worked alongside Ticketmaster F1 social and daily Stories. AE is left as supplied rather than expanded into an unverified team name.

Evidence boundary. The replay is not connected to a production system. The dots, stage changes, message wording and sounds are illustrative. The ticket status snapshots do not establish campaign reach, causal conversion, or support reduction. The mix remains incomplete at race day deliberately: no universal readiness result is implied.
